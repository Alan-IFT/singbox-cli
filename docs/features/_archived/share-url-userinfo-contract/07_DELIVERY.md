### What the pipeline established, briefly

- **The bug was real and universal in its class.** `urlparse().username` stops at the first raw colon, so `parse_tuic`'s `if ":" in userinfo:` was structurally dead and every tuic outbound `sc` ever emitted carried `"password": ""` (R-42). Measured at HEAD: all five tuic fixtures red.
- **Stage 1 refuted three clauses of its own task brief** — the seventh consecutive pool row where that held. The brief's reference implementation, `parse_ss`, was itself a member of the defect family (it decodes for **all three** arms, including base64-recovered material); the "four call sites, three wrong" count was five and four; and the storage question resolved to **re-add, not regenerate**.
- **Rule 85 was tested, not asserted.** The architect named the smaller design it rejected; the gate reconstructed that design line by line, confirmed it is **correct** on every FR, and approved the larger one only because it alone satisfies FR-1/AC-10 — then proved the three-value return minimal in both directions. Recorded in `.harness/rejected-decisions.md`.
- **The R-22 trap was closed structurally, then attacked.** AC-1 byte-compares the emitted document's credential against the constant the URL was written to carry, and AC-2 is its negative control with a void condition implemented **in code**. QA rebuilt the corpus with different constants and killed **four forbidden implementations**, each dying on exactly the fixture its condition predicts.
- **A downstream stage corrected the gate and the gate sustained it in writing** (T-07/CR-5 precedent, second occurrence): BND-4's permitted-hit enumeration was short by one, which would have produced a spurious FR-1 violation against correct code.

## Insight

- 2026-08-15 · A real `sing-box check` **accepts a tuic outbound whose `password` is the empty string** — measured on both checkouts of T-22's differential (`cand accepted {"password":"a:b:c"}` / `head accepted {"password":""}`) — so no configuration-level validation, and no `sing-box check`-based criterion, can ever detect a lost credential; only a live handshake or a byte-compare against the share URL can · evidence: share-url-userinfo-contract
- 2026-08-15 · `parse_ss`'s SIP002 arm is selected by a `ValueError` from the **colon split inside its `try`**, not by base64 validity — `_b64dec` succeeds on ordinary plaintext method names — so a colonless plaintext userinfo fell through to the `except` arm and raised a *second*, uncaught `ValueError` there, printing `Error: not enough values to unpack` · evidence: share-url-userinfo-contract
- 2026-08-15 · `.harness/scripts/verify_all.sh` resolves its checks through **relative** paths, so run from any subdirectory it self-reports `PASS 4 / FAIL 4 / SKIP 10` — a false red that looks like a product regression and is purely the caller's cwd; it must be invoked from the repository root · evidence: share-url-userinfo-contract
