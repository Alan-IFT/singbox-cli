### The safety spine — the part R-9 was deferred five times over

A committed step means `bin/sc` is imported on the owner's live machine on **every future `verify_all` run, forever**. Three properties make that safe, and each was attacked rather than asserted:

1. **The import-time auto-elevate is defused without touching `bin/sc`'s source** (the `sys.modules` `os`-shim recipe), so a refactor of the elevate block cannot defeat it.
2. **The denial is of the capability, not of the predicate.** The gate found (F-1) that the inherited recipe claim *"fails closed if `geteuid` moves"* is **false** — the shim copies the real `os.__dict__`, so `os.execvp` stays live and only the guard's predicate is falsified. That claim is now gone from `docs/dev-map.md`, replaced by "what it guarantees and what it does not". The reviewer then found (CR-1) that the first fix was **a name prefix standing in for a capability** — the same substitution one level up — and the delivered filter enumerates every POSIX process-start name in `dir(os)`.
3. **Everything else fails closed**: refusal under euid 0 at both `main()`'s and `load()`'s first statement (before `open()`, proved by making the source absent); an assertion that **every** `Path`-valued module attribute resolves inside the run's `mkdtemp` root **before** any assertion runs; the real `os` restored in a `finally` and the restoration asserted on both the clean and the raising path; a host witness over `/etc/sing-box` + entries and `/var/lib/sing-box` on **both** exit paths; and zero child processes.

**The measurement that makes this real rather than argued** is stage 6's control: under the round-1 prefix-only filter, a `bin/sc` whose elevate guard calls `os.popen` **actually started a shell** — the probe artifact exists on disk — while the suite reported `14 defined, 14 run, 14 passed`, **exit 0**.
